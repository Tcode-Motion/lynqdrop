Engineered for Speed, Built for Privacy

Everything you need for seamless cross-platform sharing without surveillance or subscriptions.

Line-Speed LAN Transfers

Transfers stream directly peer-to-peer across your local Wi-Fi or Ethernet at up to hundreds of megabytes per second. No cloud bottlenecks or artificial speed limits.

Zero-Install Browser Sharing

Share with friends or coworkers who don't have LynqDrop installed. They simply scan a QR code with their camera and download or upload files straight in their web browser.

Hotspot Broadcast Discovery

Unlike standard mDNS tools that fail on mobile hotspots, LynqDrop includes UDP broadcast fallbacks so devices discover each other effortlessly even when tethering in the field.

End-to-End Local Security

Every session uses ephemeral TLS 1.3 self-signed certificates. Your data never touches a remote server, third-party database, or advertising network.

Folders & Gigabyte Files

Stream individual files, full directory hierarchies, or 50GB videos without memory exhaustion or crash loops. True streaming chunk architecture handles any file size.

Zero Ads, Zero Telemetry

No analytics trackers, no advertising SDKs, no registration, and no premium paywalls. 100% transparent, free, open-source software under the Apache 2.0 license.

Security by Architecture

We believe privacy is an architectural invariant, not a marketing promise.

Ephemeral TLS Encryption

Every LynqDrop session generates fresh in-memory self-signed X.509 certificates. Your files are encrypted across the airwaves using modern TLS 1.3 ciphers.

Strict Local Boundary

All data travels strictly over your local network interfaces. Files are never stored in a third-party cloud, relay buffer, or external server.

No Unnecessary Permissions

LynqDrop never requests audio recording, contacts, SMS, or broad package scanning permissions. Storage access follows modern Scoped Storage guidelines.

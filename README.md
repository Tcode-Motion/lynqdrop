## ✨ Features

- **Device-to-Device Sharing**: Transfer photos, 4K videos, large archives, and entire nested folders directly between Android, Windows, macOS, and Linux over local Wi-Fi or portable hotspots.
- **Zero-Install Browser Sharing**: Share files with friends or coworkers who don't have LynqDrop installed. They simply scan a QR code with their camera to download or upload files straight in their web browser.
- **Zero Cloud Reliance**: Your files never touch external servers or third-party cloud storage. Transfers stream directly over your local subnet at full Wi-Fi speeds (up to 80+ MB/s).
- **Ephemeral TLS Encryption**: Every session generates fresh in-memory TLS certificates to encrypt transfers over the airwaves.
- **No Accounts, No Tracking**: 100% private. No logins, no analytics SDKs, and zero telemetry.
